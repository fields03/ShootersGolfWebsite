Privacy Policy

How Shooters Golf collects, uses, and protects information when you use our golf scoring application.

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Shooters Golf ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our golf scoring application.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password
  • User Profile: Your display name and email address are stored in your user profile
  • Authentication Data: We use Firebase Authentication to securely manage your login credentials

Game Data

  • Golf Scores: Your individual hole scores, total scores, and game statistics
  • Player Information: Names, handicaps, and favorite status of players you add
  • Course Data: Golf course information including hole details, par values, and handicap ratings
  • Game History: Past games, dates played, and game results
  • Share Codes: Unique codes generated for sharing games with other players

Communication Data

  • Chat Messages: Text messages sent in game chat features
  • Chat Images: Photos shared in game chat conversations
  • Message Metadata: Timestamps and sender information for chat messages

Device and Usage Information

  • Device Information: Basic device information for app functionality
  • App Usage: How you interact with the app features
  • Local Storage: Cached data stored on your device for offline functionality

How We Use Your Information

Core App Functionality

  • Account Management: To create and manage your user account
  • Game Scoring: To track and display your golf scores and statistics
  • Player Management: To store and manage player information and handicaps
  • Course Management: To provide golf course information and hole details
  • Game Sharing: To enable sharing games with other players via share codes
  • Chat Features: To facilitate communication between players during games

Service Improvement

  • App Performance: To improve app functionality and user experience
  • Bug Fixes: To identify and resolve technical issues
  • Feature Development: To develop new features based on usage patterns

Communication

  • Password Reset: To send password reset emails when requested
  • Account Notifications: To provide important account-related information

Data Storage and Security

Cloud Storage

  • Firebase Firestore: Your game data, player information, and chat messages are stored securely in Google's Firebase Firestore database
  • Firebase Storage: Chat images are stored in Firebase Storage
  • Firebase Authentication: Your login credentials are managed securely through Firebase Authentication

Local Storage

  • Device Cache: Some data is cached locally on your device for offline access
  • UserDefaults: App preferences and settings are stored locally

Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data transmitted to and from our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Authentication: Secure login processes protect your account
  • Access Controls: Data access is restricted to authorized users only

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise distribute your personal information to any third parties for marketing, advertising, or commercial purposes. Your data is used solely for the purposes described in this privacy policy to provide you with our golf scoring services.

Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Services: We use Google Firebase for data storage, authentication, and hosting. Firebase is operated by Google and subject to Google's privacy policy. This is necessary for the app's core functionality.
  • Google Services: Firebase is operated by Google and subject to Google's privacy policy

Game Sharing

  • Share Codes: When you share a game, other players can access the game data using the share code you provide
  • Chat Messages: Messages and images in game chats are visible to all players in that game

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

Your Rights and Choices

Account Management

  • Update Information: You can update your profile information through the app
  • Delete Account: You can delete your account and associated data
  • Logout: You can log out of your account at any time

Data Access

  • View Your Data: You can view your game history and player information in the app
  • Export Data: Contact us to request a copy of your data

Communication Preferences

  • Email Notifications: You can control password reset emails through your account settings

Data Retention

Active Data

  • Game Data: Your games and scores are retained while your account is active
  • Chat Messages: Chat messages are retained for 14 days, then automatically deleted
  • User Profile: Your profile information is retained while your account exists

Deleted Data

  • Account Deletion: When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed
  • Chat Cleanup: Old chat messages and images are automatically deleted after 14 days

Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy in the app or sending you an email.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, contact us through the Shooters Golf app, or update this page with your preferred support email when you publish the site.

California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Please contact us for more information about these rights.

European Privacy Rights

If you are in the European Union, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Please contact us for more information about these rights.

This privacy policy applies to the Shooters Golf app and all related services. By using our app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.
