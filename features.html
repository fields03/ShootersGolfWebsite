Features

Everything your group needs on the course.

From classic formats to live sharing — built around how golfers actually bet, team up, and keep score.

Game formats

Run the formats your group already plays — without paper, calculators, or post-round arguments.

How it works

One setup. One share code. Everyone stays in sync.

Live scoring

Hole-by-hole updates for the whole group

Enter scores as you play. Teammates and opponents see the leaderboard update in real time — on iPhone or in the browser.

Share codes

Invite anyone with a short code

Start a game, share the code, and co-owners can join the round and follow live results without messy invite chains.

Handicaps

Course handicaps that match the tee

Use player indexes — including GHIN when linked — and recalculate playing handicaps for the course and tee you’re on.

Courses

Par, slope, and hole handicaps

Keep course and tee data with the round so net scores, skins, and strokes received stay accurate all eighteen holes.

Players

Your regulars, ready to go

Save players, mark favorites, and pull them into the next game without retyping names and indexes every Saturday.

History

Past rounds when you need them

Look back at finished games, results, and who showed up — useful for settling presses or bragging rights later.

Chat

Talk trash between holes

In-game chat keeps the group connected when you’re spread across the fairway — text and photos included.

Accounts

Signed in, synced, and yours

Firebase-backed accounts keep your players, games, and history available across devices when you’re signed in.
